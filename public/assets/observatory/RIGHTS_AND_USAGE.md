## Edits and file

The supplied MP4 uses a 4.2-second excerpt beginning at 3.1 seconds in the 720p/50 broadcast source. It excludes logo cards, removes all audio, and slows motion to half speed. The encoding is H.264/yuv420p in MP4 with faststart, 1280 × 720, 25 fps, approximately 8.4 seconds. No color alteration or synthetic replacement imagery has been added.

The excerpt is not a seamless loop. A soft transition can be added by the website if desired. Respect reduced-motion settings and stop playback when the overlay closes.
