## What the footage shows

This is an astronomical zoom from a photographic panorama of the Milky Way toward its central region. It is not live telescope footage, an Earth-view time-lapse, or an AI-generated scene. The source video eventually reaches an infrared HAWK-I observation; this replacement uses only the opening, recognizable visible-light Milky Way band and core.

## Edits

The MP4 excerpts 0.4–10.4 seconds of the source, slows it to half speed, removes audio, and encodes H.264/yuv420p at 960 × 540, 25 fps, approximately 20 seconds with faststart. No color or brightness filter, generated content, or fake astronomy was added. The poster is the source frame at 4 seconds. The web app may center-crop to a circle; the contact sheet verifies the Milky Way core and band remain visible in center square crops. This is not a seamless loop.
