# Tarot artwork and reflection credits

## Illustrations

This website uses all 78 illustrations from the Rider–Waite–Smith tarot deck, illustrated by **Pamela Colman Smith**, with the deck's conception and symbolism developed with **Arthur Edward Waite**, originally published by Rider in 1909–1910.

The source is Wikimedia Commons' complete **TaionWC Pam-A scan set**:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite-Smith_tarot_deck_(TaionWC)

Every one of the 78 image records was checked on 6 November 2026. Each returned `LicenseShortName: Public domain`, `UsageTerms: Public domain`, and `AttributionRequired: false`. The local `manifest.json` records each card's exact description page, original image URL, downloaded thumbnail URL, card identity, source checksum, and local checksum.

The artwork is locally hosted as 500-pixel-wide JPEG thumbnails supplied by Wikimedia Commons. Original imagery and colors have not been deliberately altered. The set contains 22 Major Arcana and 56 Minor Arcana, with 14 cards each in Wands, Cups, Swords, and Pentacles. Strength is VIII and Justice XI. In each suit, 11 is Page, 12 Knight, 13 Queen, and 14 King.

Some historical illustrations contain nonsexual artistic nudity and symbolic depictions of death or injury. They are retained as part of the original deck.

## Reflections

The short messages and actions in `abundance-card-data.js` were newly written for this implementation as upright-oriented, non-predictive reflection prompts inspired by the cards' familiar themes. They are not copied from a modern tarot guidebook, not quotations from Waite, and not represented as Carla's own authored interpretation. They do not provide health, financial, legal, or other professional advice and do not predict outcomes. Reversed interpretations are not provided.
