Meet Carla Welch

Carla founded The Awakened Academy as a place where people can slow down and hear themselves think. As a guide and facilitator, she doesn’t promise outcomes or quick fixes — she offers presence, thoughtful questions, and room to explore at your own pace.

Her work centers on a simple belief: given a little space and honest company, people are remarkably good at finding their own way forward.
