Put it into practice

The Helpful Creation Plan gives you one working page for your idea, purpose, audience, support and next action. A seven-day practice helps you explore the process at your own pace. No grand launch or finished business is required.

one old sentence.

You do not need to finish the book before beginning the reflection. Notice the thought that appears just before you set an idea aside.
