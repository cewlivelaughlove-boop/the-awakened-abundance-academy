Start Here: Make Room for
Your Own Voice

A quiet 3–5-minute practice.

You don’t have to change your whole life today. Begin by noticing one belief, imagining another possibility, and choosing one helpful action.

01

Step 1: Notice what
you inherited

Take a breath. Think of something you were taught about success, money, work, or who you’re allowed to become.

One belief I learned is ____.

Does it still reflect what I value—or am I ready to question it?

You can appreciate where you came from without keeping every expectation. Honest work and everyday responsibilities are not signs that you’ve failed.

02

Step 2: Imagine what
you could create

Make a little room for your imagination. Consider the gifts, curiosity, and creative potential you carry—and how you might use them in connection with the Creator.

If I gave myself permission to begin small, I would create ____.

It could help someone by ____.

Money can support that work. It is a tool, not the measure of your worth. Start with something useful, caring, or meaningful.

03

Step 3: Choose one
helpful action

Pick something you can do today in ten minutes or less: sketch an idea, write a paragraph, practice a skill, or ask someone what would genuinely help them.

Today, I will ____.

I’ll make space for it at ____.

Before you go

What feels more like my own voice now—and what will I do with it?

You’ve taken a first step. Beyond the Script explores these questions further: the beliefs we inherit and the possibilities we can begin to choose.
